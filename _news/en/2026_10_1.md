Our work showing that universal coding over classical-quantum channels can fall short of the optimal reliability achievable with channel knowledge has been posted on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01941). Joint work with Masahito Hayashi, Takaya Matsuura, and Hao-Chung Cheng.
