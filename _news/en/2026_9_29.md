Our work showing how classical correlations between measurement outcomes can certify the performance of entanglement distillation and quantum error correction without assuming measurement complementarity has been posted on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38168). Joint work with Yota Tachibana, Yoshifumi Nakata, Takaya Matsuura, Francesco Buscemi, and Masato Koashi.
