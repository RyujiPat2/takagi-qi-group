Our work developing methods to assess stellar rank, a measure of non-Gaussianity, from Wigner-function measurements and applying them to experimental data from superconducting cavities has been posted on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40827). Joint work with Alex Maltesson, Varun Upreti, Marike Ellerbroek, Alessandro Ferraro, Saleh Rahimi-Keshari, Ulysse Chabaud, and Giulia Ferrini.
